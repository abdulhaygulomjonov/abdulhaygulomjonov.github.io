Ibn Sina Academy

Ibn Sina Academy — kundalik vazifalar, sanalar, jadvallar va vaqt vositalarini bir joyda yuritish uchun Android ilova. Google akkaunt bilan kirsangiz, ma’lumotlaringiz o’zingizning Google Drive’ingizdagi yashirin papkada zaxiralanadi va yangi telefonda tiklanadi.

Ilova faqat email manzilingiz va Drive “appDataFolder” ruxsatini so’raydi.

Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

Ibn Sina Academy is an Android app for keeping tasks, dates, tables and time tools in one place. When you sign in with Google, your data is backed up to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive so you can restore it on a new phone. The app requests only your email address and the Drive “appDataFolder” permission.
